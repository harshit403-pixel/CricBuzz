# Initial Feature Scope

This document lists possible feature modules for the project. It is only an initial scope draft and should not be treated as the final task assignment.

## Authentication

- May include login and logout flow for admin or scorer roles
- May help protect restricted panels and update actions
- May later expand into role-based access if needed

## Matches

- May include upcoming, live, and completed match listings
- May include match summary details such as date, venue, and teams
- May provide entry points to scorecards and live updates

## Live Score

- May show current score, overs, wickets, and match status
- May support refresh-based or real-time updates
- May focus on simple readable presentation in the early stage

## Teams

- May include team profiles and basic team information
- May show squad members and team-related statistics later
- May connect with match and points table sections

## Players

- May include player profiles and basic details
- May show role, team association, and simple performance information
- May expand later based on available data and project scope

## Scorecard

- May include batting and bowling summaries
- May show innings-level score details
- May connect closely with match and live score modules

## Points Table

- May display team standings in a tournament format
- May include points, wins, losses, and net run rate if needed
- May start with mock or static data during early development

## News and Updates

- May include announcements, match highlights, or important updates
- May help keep public users informed from the homepage or a separate section
- May begin with static content before any admin-managed flow

## Admin or Scorer Panel

- May allow controlled updates to match and score-related data
- May include forms for match creation or score editing
- May later connect with authentication and permission handling

## Out of Scope for Initial Planning

- Exact API endpoints
- Final database schema
- Complete UI design
- Deployment pipeline
- Production-level socket logic

## Final Note

This feature scope is meant to support early planning only. It can be updated after mentor instructions, team discussion, and official task assignments.
